I develop architectural foundations for Functional Self-Awareness in AI systems, with emphasis on system-level reliability, bounded authority, and governance interfaces for autonomous operation.

About

My work introduces the Functional Self-Awareness Architecture (FSAA), a systems engineering framework comprising persistent self-modeling, uncertainty-awareness mechanisms, meta-cognitive monitoring, and self-governance interfaces. The goal is to enable AI systems that can regulate their own authority based on reliability signals and operational constraints.
